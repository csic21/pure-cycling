## Compatibility and operational guard

Old shared-name uploads and unsafe direct cloud wipes are blocked. Local recording and reading previously committed objects remain available; update the app to restore writes. This protects newer immutable upload attempts and conflict winners.

The Storage finalization trigger is a deliberate managed-schema compatibility exception. It does not directly modify/delete Storage records; cleanup uses the Storage API. Re-run `Storage cleanup regression` against the planned Storage/Postgres versions before platform upgrades. The regression verifies raw-byte reclamation after late-upload rejection, not merely disappearance of metadata.
